## Overview

The Dashboard is the project-wide view of calling activity: a conversion funnel from attempted to interested, a per-process performance table, and a breakdown of dispositions and outcomes. It has two tabs — **Overview** (this page) and **Lead Journey** (cohort-based analytics for a single process, see below).

Filter by **process** (or "All processes") and **date range** at the top — every section on the page updates to match.

## Conversion Funnel

The top-level funnel for the selected date range and process filter:

- **Attempted** — leads dialed at least once.
- **Connected** — attempts where the customer picked up, shown as a percentage of Attempted.
- **Interested** — connected leads that expressed interest, shown as a percentage of Attempted.
- **Incoming** — inbound calls received in the same window (not part of the outbound funnel, shown separately).

The bars below the cards give the same numbers as a quick visual comparison.

## Performance Matrix

A per-process breakdown table: **Attempted**, **Connected**, **Interested**, **Lost**, and **Avg. Attempts** (average call attempts per lead) for every process active in the selected window. Use the row action to drill into a specific process's analytics — see [Process Analytics](/essentials/process-analytics).

## Disposition Breakdown

Dispositions across all processes in the window, grouped into three outcome categories:

- **Success** — e.g. Retail, Booking Request.
- **Neutral** — e.g. Inconclusive, No Connect, Test Drive Requested.
- **Failure** — e.g. Lost - Not Purchased, Wrong Number, Lost to Co-dealer.

Each row shows the count and its share of the applicable total. Exact disposition names are process-specific — these are examples from a live project, not a fixed list.

## Attempt Outcomes & Disposition Type Distribution

Two donut charts summarizing the same window from different angles:

- **Attempt Outcomes** — the share of individual call attempts that connected vs. didn't, across all attempts (not just first attempts).
- **Disposition Type Distribution** — the share of dispositions that landed in Success, Neutral, or Failure.

## Attempt-wise Summary

A bar chart breaking down outcomes (Not Connected / Connected / Success) by attempt number (Attempt 1 through Attempt 10, paginated), showing how connect and success rates change as retries increase.

<Tip>
Every card and table on this page has a **Download Leads** or export option in its header — use it to pull the underlying lead list for any section instead of just the aggregate numbers.
</Tip>

## Lead Journey Tab

The **Lead Journey** tab requires picking a single process and opens **Cohort View** — leads grouped by insertion date rather than by calendar day, with heatmaps for calling activity and conversion, plus a calling-velocity table. See [Cohort View (Lead Journey)](/guides/process/cohort-view) for the full breakdown.

Use the Dashboard's Overview tab for project-wide, calendar-date performance, and Lead Journey / Cohort View or [Process Analytics](/essentials/process-analytics) when you need to reason about a single process's leads by the day they entered it.
