## Overview

A **lead** is a contact enrolled into a process — it carries contact details, custom metadata, and a running history of every call attempt, disposition, and state transition. This page is a starting point; each linked guide below covers one part of the leads workflow in detail.

## Adding Leads

- **[How to Upload Leads File](/guides/leads/how-to-upload-leads-file)** — bulk upload via CSV.
- **[Upload Test Lead](/essentials/upload-test-lead)** — send a single test lead through a process before a real campaign.
- **API** — create or update leads programmatically; see [API Integration](/essentials/api-integration).

Minimum recommended fields: name, phone, timezone. Optional: email, company, notes, campaign tags, and any custom metadata your process uses for routing or personalization.

## Finding Leads

- **[How to Search in Leads Dashboard](/guides/leads/how-to-search-in-leads-dashboard)** — filter by lead fields, metadata, and state using the boolean AND/OR query builder.
- **[Lead Queue Prioritization](/guides/leads/lead-queue-prioritization)** — control which leads get called first within a process.

## Exporting and Bulk Operations

- **[How to Download Interested Leads](/guides/leads/how-to-download-interested-leads)** — filter to Interested leads and export to CSV, including full vs. masked phone number export.
- **[Bulk Lead Operations](/guides/leads/bulk-lead-operations)** — delete, close-lost, reset, or reschedule leads in bulk across a filtered set.

## Best Practices

- Normalize phone numbers in E.164 format.
- Provide timezone for optimal call windows.
- Use a stable external/reference ID to keep your CRM and Uservox in sync.
