## What Is a Pulse?

A **pulse** measures connected call duration on Uservox. Every connected call (the customer actually picked up) is billed in pulses:

- **1 pulse = 1 minute of connected talk time**
- Duration rounds **up** to the next full minute — a 45-second call is 1 pulse, a 70-second call is 2 pulses.

Pulses are the primary usage metric for billing and capacity planning — they reflect actual connected talk-time, not total call attempts (unconnected calls consume no pulses).

## How to Reach Pulse Usage

1. Click your profile in the top-right corner.
2. Select **Account Settings**.
3. From the left sidebar, click **Pulses**.

![Account Settings profile menu, showing the Pulses entry in the left sidebar](/images/Screenshotfrom2025-10-3100-29-01.png)

This is currently the only path to pulse usage — it isn't surfaced elsewhere in the main navigation.
