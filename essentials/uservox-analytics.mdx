### The Two Views (with the numbers explained)

Uservox Analytics shows the same data through two views. They look similar but answer opposite questions, because each is anchored on a different date:

- **Dashboard** is anchored on the **call attempt date** — when a call was made.
- **Leads Dashboard** is anchored on the **lead insertion date** — when a lead was added.

Choose the view that matches the question you're asking.

---

## 1. Dashboard (Activity view)

- **Anchored on:** the call attempt date
- **Answers:** "How much calling did we do, and how did it convert?"
- **Counts:** every lead touched in the time window, no matter when it was added (a lead uploaded weeks ago but dialed today is counted today).

![Screenshot 2026 09 10 At 2 24 36 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-09-10-at-2.24.36-PM.png "Screenshot 2026 09 10 At 2 24 36 PM")

### What the numbers in this snapshot mean

**Conversion Funnel**

- **Attempted — 630 (100%):** 630 leads received at least one call attempt in the window. This is the base for every percentage in the funnel.
- **Connected — 397 (63%):** of those 630, 397 answered. 397 ÷ 630 = 63%.
- **Interested — 41 (7%):** 41 of the attempted leads expressed interest. 41 ÷ 630 ≈ 7%.
- **Incoming — 58:** 58 inbound calls were received. This is a separate inbound counter, not part of the outbound funnel.

**Performance Matrix** — the same funnel broken down by process. The rows add up to the funnel totals:

| Process | Attempted | Connected | Interested | Lost | Avg. Attempts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Process 1 | 371 | 186 | 11 | 78 | 4.48 |
| Process 2 | 150 | 123 | 17 | 30 | 4.35 |
| Process 3 | 109 | 88 | 13 | 21 | 4.42 |
| **Total** | **630** | **397** | **41** | **129** | — |

- **Attempted / Connected / Interested** sum exactly to the funnel (630 / 397 / 41), confirming the funnel is just the total of these three processes.
- **Avg. Attempts ~4.4** means each lead was dialed roughly four to five times on average before an outcome.

**Disposition Breakdown (Success)**

- **Interested_Hot — 17** and **Interested_Warm — 13:** the specific tags behind the "interested" outcomes recorded in this activity window.

**Attempt Outcomes**

- **27% connected:** this is measured **per dial**, not per lead. Because each lead is dialed ~4.4 times, only about 27% of individual dials connect — even though 63% of leads eventually connect across all their attempts.

### Use it to

- Track daily or weekly throughput.
- Check the connect rate on calls actually made.
- Spot processes using too many attempts per lead.
- Watch inbound call volume.

## 2. Leads Dashboard (Cohort view)

- **Anchored on:** the lead insertion date
- **Answers:** "How is the batch of leads we acquired performing?"
- **Counts:** a fixed cohort of leads by upload date, tracked over time — regardless of when the calls behind those outcomes happened.

![Screenshot 2026 09 10 At 2 24 51 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-09-10-at-2.24.51-PM.png "Screenshot 2026 09 10 At 2 24 51 PM")

### What the numbers in this snapshot mean

**Summary** — everything here is a share of the 540-lead cohort (leads inserted 3–10 Sept):

- **All leads — 540:** the total inserted in the range. This is the base.
- **Connected — 294 (54.4%):** 294 of the 540 have connected at least once. 294 ÷ 540 = 54.4%.
- **Interested — 35 (6.5%):** 35 expressed interest. 35 ÷ 540 = 6.5%.
- **Lost — 97 (18.0%):** 97 are closed as lost. 97 ÷ 540 = 18.0%.
- **In process — 408 (75.6%):** 408 are still active in the pipeline. This overlaps with Connected — a lead can be connected _and_ still in process — which is why these figures don't add to 100%.

**Top dispositions** — each lead's current disposition, as a share of the 540:

- **DND — 142 (26.3%):** largest bucket; numbers on the do-not-disturb list.
- **Call_Later — 111 (20.6%):** asked to be called back later.
- **None — 106 (19.6%):** no disposition yet — not reached so far.
- **Lost – Not to Be Contacted — 45 (8.3%)**
- **Lost_Not_Interested — 39 (7.2%)**
- **Last_Call_Not_Connected — 32 (5.9%)**
- **Interested_Hot — 18 (3.3%)**
- **Interested_Warm — 15 (2.8%)**

**States**

- **None — 142 (26.3%):** leads that haven't advanced into a pipeline state yet.

The tabs at the top mirror the summary: **All 540 · Interested 35 · Connected 294 · Lost 97.**

### Use it to

- Judge the quality of an acquired batch (DND vs interested).
- See how far a cohort has moved (in process vs lost).
- Pull the working list of leads with their next action.

## Why the same label shows different numbers

The two snapshots deliberately disagree, because they count different populations over different date anchors. For example:

| Metric | Dashboard (activity) | Leads Dashboard (cohort) | Why they differ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Interested | 41 | 35 | Activity in the window vs current state of the inserted cohort |
| Interested_Hot | 17 | 18 | Same reason — different base and time anchor |
| Interested_Warm | 13 | 15 | Same reason |
| Connect rate | 63% (of dialed) | 54.4% (of cohort) | Different denominator entirely |

This is expected. The numbers are not errors — they're two different questions being answered.

---

## Reading the connect rates

"Connected" appears in both views but is measured against a different base each time, so the percentages are not meant to match — and should not be compared directly:

- **63%** — Dashboard funnel — of leads _dialed_ in the window.
- **27%** — Dashboard donut — of individual _attempts_.
- **54.4%** — Leads Dashboard — of leads _inserted_ in the range.

---

## In short

- The **Dashboard** is about **throughput** — what you did.
- The **Leads Dashboard** is about **cohort quality** — what you got.
- One lead can appear in both: it joins the Leads Dashboard the day it's uploaded and stays in that cohort, and it shows on the Dashboard whichever day it's actually dialed.
