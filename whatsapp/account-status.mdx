## Overview

Meta reviews several things about your WhatsApp number: your business, the display name customers see, the quality of your messages, and whether you have the blue tick. Uservox reads these from Meta and shows them in one place, so you don't need to check WhatsApp Manager.

Uservox also checks every day and emails your project admins when something changes, for example when Meta approves a new display name or your quality rating drops.

---

## Open the status panel

1. Open [**WhatsApp → Settings**](https://app.uservox.ai/whatsapp/settings).
2. Find the **Display name and blue tick** panel, below your WhatsApp account details.

Each row has a label that shows where it stands:

| Label | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Done** | Nothing to do |
| **In progress** | Meta is reviewing it, or a requirement isn't met yet |
| **Action needed** | Something needs your attention |
| **Unknown** | Uservox couldn't read it from Meta. See [If a row shows Unknown](#if-a-row-shows-unknown) |

The status is refreshed when you open the page if it is more than an hour old. Click **Check with Meta** to read it again right away.

<Note>
Everyone in the project can see the panel. Only **project admins** can change the display name or apply for the blue tick.
</Note>

## What each row means

### Meta business verification

Whether Meta has confirmed that your business is real. Without it, you can message fewer customers a day and you can't get the blue tick.

If it isn't verified, start or finish verification in Meta Business Suite under **Settings → Security Center**. See [Verify your business](/whatsapp/prepare-business#step-3-verify-your-business).

### Display name

The business name customers see on your messages. The row shows one of these:

- **The approved name**, such as *"Acme Finance" is approved*.
- **A new name in review**, with the current name still in use while Meta reviews it.
- **A declined name.** Choose a name closer to your business name and submit it again.

To change the name, click **Change display name**. See [Change your display name](/whatsapp/change-display-name).

### Quality rating

How customers are responding to your messages, based on blocks and reports over the last 7 days:

- **Green**: high quality.
- **Yellow**: medium quality. Review which templates customers block or report.
- **Red**: low quality. Pause or rework marketing templates. If it stays red, Meta can lower your messaging limit.

### Messaging limit

How many different customers you can start conversations with in 24 hours, such as *1,000 customers a day*. Meta raises it on its own as you send more messages with good quality. Replies to customers who message you first don't count toward it.

### Blue tick

Whether your number has the blue tick (Official Business Account):

- **Done**: your number has the blue tick.
- **In progress**: a request is in review with Meta, or your number doesn't meet Meta's requirements yet. The row lists what is missing, for example *The number must be on the platform for 30 days*.
- **Action needed**: your number meets the requirements. Click **Apply for blue tick** to send a request.

See [Apply for the blue tick](/whatsapp/display-name-and-blue-tick#apply-for-the-blue-tick) for what Meta looks for.

## Email alerts

Uservox checks every connected number once a day. When something has changed since the last check, it emails the project admins. You get an email when:

- Meta approves or declines a new display name
- Meta approves or rejects a blue tick request
- your business verification status changes
- your quality rating goes up or down
- your messaging limit changes

The email lists what changed and links to the WhatsApp Settings page. A change you see with **Check with Meta** is emailed in the same way, so admins hear about it once whoever finds it first.

<Tip>
To make sure the right people get these emails, make them **admins** of the project under **Settings → Members**.
</Tip>

---

## If a row shows Unknown

**Unknown** means Meta didn't return that value when Uservox asked. This can happen for a short time while Meta's systems are busy. Click **Check with Meta** again after a few minutes.

If a row stays **Unknown**, or the panel says no Meta access is stored for your number, contact the Uservox team.
