## Overview

Your **display name** is the business name customers see on your WhatsApp messages and profile. You set it when you [connected WhatsApp](/whatsapp/connect-whatsapp). You can change it later from Uservox.

Every new name goes to Meta for review, usually for 1 to 2 working days. Customers keep seeing your current name until Meta approves the new one. Uservox emails your project admins when Meta decides.

<Note>
Changing the display name doesn't change your number, templates, quality rating or messaging limit.
</Note>

---

## Before you start

- **You need to be a project admin** in Uservox.
- **The name should appear on your website.** Meta compares the name with your business details and website, so use your company name or a brand you own.
- **Only one name can be in review at a time.** Wait for Meta's decision before you request another.
- **Meta allows up to 10 changes in 30 days.**
- **Numbers with the blue tick** can't change their name from Uservox. Meta has to make the change, so contact the Uservox team.

---

## Request a new name

1. Open [**WhatsApp → Settings**](https://app.uservox.ai/whatsapp/settings).
2. In the **Display name and blue tick** panel, click **Change display name**.
3. Enter the new name. Uservox checks it against Meta's rules as you type and lists anything to fix.
4. Click **Send to Meta for review**.

The **Display name** row in the panel now shows the new name as **In progress**, with your current name still in use.

If the **Change display name** button isn't shown, the panel says why, for example that another name is already in review.

You can also change the name in WhatsApp Manager: go to **Phone numbers**, select your number and click **Edit** next to the display name. Uservox tracks the review either way.

---

## After Meta decides

Uservox checks with Meta every day and emails your project admins when the review is done. You can also click **Check with Meta** in the panel to check right away.

- **Approved.** The **Display name** row shows the new name as approved. It can take up to a day for customers to see it. If they still see the old name after that, contact the Uservox team and we will register the number again so the new name takes effect.
- **Declined.** The row shows **Action needed**. Choose a name that is closer to your business name and request it again.

---

## Names Meta approves

Meta approves names that have a clear link to your business.

| Usually approved | Usually declined |
| :--- | :--- |
| Your company name, such as "Acme Finance" | A generic word on its own, such as "Loans", "Hiring" or "Customer Care" |
| A brand you own that appears on your website | A brand or person you can't show a link to |
| A brand plus a clear purpose, such as "Acme Support" or "Acme Hiring" | Emojis, web addresses or the word "WhatsApp" |
| Normal capital letters, such as "Acme Finance" | All capitals or special characters, such as "ACME BEST LOANS!!" |

Uservox blocks names that Meta always declines, such as names with emojis, web addresses, "WhatsApp" or special characters like `! ? @ #`. It warns you about names in all capital letters, because Meta usually declines them unless the name is an acronym.
