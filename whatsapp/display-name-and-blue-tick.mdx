## Overview

Your WhatsApp number has two things that tell customers who you are:

- **Display name.** The business name shown on your messages and profile. You set it when you [connect WhatsApp](/whatsapp/connect-whatsapp), and you can change it later. Meta reviews every new name.
- **Blue tick (Official Business Account).** A verified badge next to your name. It shows that Meta has confirmed the account belongs to a well-known brand. Meta decides who gets it.

Both requests go to Meta. You can submit them yourself in WhatsApp Manager, or ask the Uservox team to submit them for you.

---

## Before you start

- **Finish Meta business verification.** Without it, the blue tick request is rejected. See [Verify your business](/whatsapp/prepare-business#step-3-verify-your-business).
- **Turn on two-step verification** for the number in WhatsApp Manager (**Phone numbers → your number → Two-step verification**). Keep the 6-digit PIN safe, because it is needed if the number has to be registered again.
- **Show the name on your website.** Meta checks the display name against your website and business details, so the name you ask for should appear clearly on your site.

## Change your display name

1. Sign in to [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com) and open **WhatsApp Manager**.
2. Go to **Phone numbers** and select your number.
3. Next to the display name, click **Edit**.
4. Enter the new name and submit it for review.

Meta usually reviews the name in 1 to 2 working days. While it is in review, customers keep seeing the old name. Once it is approved, the new name normally appears on its own. If it hasn't changed a day after approval, contact the Uservox team and we will register the number again.

### Names Meta approves

The name must have a clear link to your business. Meta compares it with your business details and your website.

| Usually approved | Usually rejected |
| :--- | :--- |
| Your company name, such as "Acme Finance" | Generic words on their own, such as "Loans" or "Customer Care" |
| A brand you own that appears on your website | A brand or person you can't show a link to |
| A brand plus a clear function, such as "Acme Support" | Extra keywords, emojis or unusual capital letters, such as "ACME BEST LOANS!!" |

If Meta rejects the name, it tells you why. Choose a name that is closer to your business name and submit again.

<Note>
Changing the display name does not change your number, templates or quality rating.
</Note>

## Apply for the blue tick

Apply only after your display name is approved. If you change the name later, the badge is removed and you have to apply again.

1. In **WhatsApp Manager**, go to **Phone numbers** and select your number.
2. Open the **Profile** tab.
3. Under **Official business account**, click **Submit request**.
4. Add links that show your brand is well known (below), and submit.

### What to include

Meta gives the blue tick to brands that people often search for, so the links matter most:

- **3 to 5 news articles** about your company from well-known news or industry publications. Paid, sponsored or press-release articles don't count.
- Your **official website**.
- Your **social media profiles**, such as your verified Facebook, Instagram or LinkedIn pages.

<Warning>
Meta decides at its own discretion and doesn't always give a reason for a rejection. If your request is rejected, wait **30 days** before applying again. Use that time to collect stronger news coverage.
</Warning>

### If Meta doesn't approve the blue tick

Smaller or newer brands are often turned down for the free blue tick. Meta also sells a **Meta Verified for Business** subscription that gives a verified badge after an identity check. Look for **Meta Verified** in your Meta Business Suite. It is being rolled out gradually, so it may not be available for every number yet.
