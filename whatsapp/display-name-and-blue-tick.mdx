## Overview

Your WhatsApp number has two things that tell customers who you are:

- **Display name.** The business name shown on your messages and profile. You set it when you [connect WhatsApp](/whatsapp/connect-whatsapp), and you can change it later. Meta reviews every new name.
- **Blue tick (Official Business Account).** A verified badge next to your name. It shows that Meta has confirmed the account belongs to a well-known brand. Meta decides who gets it.

Both requests go to Meta. You can send them, and see where each one stands, on the [WhatsApp Settings](https://app.uservox.ai/whatsapp/settings) page in Uservox.

---

## Before you start

- **Finish Meta business verification.** Without it, the blue tick request is rejected. See [Verify your business](/whatsapp/prepare-business#step-3-verify-your-business).
- **Turn on two-step verification** for the number in WhatsApp Manager (**Phone numbers → your number → Two-step verification**). Keep the 6-digit PIN safe, because it is needed if the number has to be registered again.
- **Show the name on your website.** Meta checks the display name against your website and business details, so the name you ask for should appear clearly on your site.
- **Wait 30 days after connecting.** Meta only accepts blue tick requests for numbers that have been on the WhatsApp Business Platform for at least 30 days.

## Change your display name

In the **Display name and blue tick** panel on [**WhatsApp → Settings**](https://app.uservox.ai/whatsapp/settings), click **Change display name**, enter the new name and send it to Meta. Meta usually reviews it in 1 to 2 working days, and Uservox emails your project admins when it decides.

See [Change your display name](/whatsapp/change-display-name) for the steps and the names Meta approves.

## Apply for the blue tick

Apply only after your display name is approved. If you change the name later, Meta reviews the account again and may remove the badge.

1. Open [**WhatsApp → Settings**](https://app.uservox.ai/whatsapp/settings) in Uservox. You need to be a project admin.
2. In the **Display name and blue tick** panel, click **Apply for blue tick**. The button appears once your number meets Meta's requirements. Until then, the panel lists what is missing.
3. Enter your website, main country and language, and the links to articles about your business.
4. Click **Send request to Meta**.

You can also apply in **WhatsApp Manager**: go to **Phone numbers**, select your number, open the **Profile** tab and click **Submit request** under **Official business account**.

### What to include

Meta gives the blue tick to brands that people often search for, so the articles matter most:

- **5 to 10 links to news articles** about your company from well-known news or industry publications, ideally from the last 12 months.
- Paid or sponsored articles and press releases don't count.
- Links to your own website or social media pages don't count as supporting links. Enter your website in its own field.

<Warning>
Meta decides at its own discretion and doesn't always give a reason for a rejection. If your request is rejected, wait **30 days** before applying again. Use that time to collect stronger news coverage.
</Warning>

### If Meta doesn't approve the blue tick

Smaller or newer brands are often turned down for the free blue tick. Meta also sells a **Meta Verified for Business** subscription that gives a verified badge after an identity check. Look for **Meta Verified** in your Meta Business Suite. It is being rolled out gradually, so it may not be available for every number yet.
